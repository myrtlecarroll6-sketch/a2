Inside the KnitFootprint Atelier: where 15.5-micron Saxon fleece, Italian 200-needle circular knitting beds, and hand-linked seamless toes unite in golden luxury.

At KnitFootprint, our maison is devoted to the biomechanical engineering, worsted spinning metallurgy, and bench craftsmanship of haute luxury knitwear and merino wool hosiery. Rejecting synthetic polyester fast fashion, our atelier builds on centuries of European knitting mastery: hand-selecting sub-16-micron Saxon fleece, spinning high-twist worsted yarns, and linking toe seams loop-by-loop by hand on radial dial beds.

From calibrating high-modulus Lycra arch bands that cradle the plantar fascia to finishing cashmere socks with natural wool lanolin steam boarding, our research monographs and bespoke commissions elevate daily foundation garments into wearable architectural luxury.

Our Core Knitwear Disciplines

1. Saxon Superfine Merino & Cashmere Sourcing: We select only long-staple fibers under 15.5 microns with high natural spiral crimp, providing natural vapor thermoregulation and itch-free softness.
2. 200-Needle Single-Cylinder Bed Knitting: We deploy high-density circular knitting machines to achieve dense, opaque, high-recovery fabrics that never sag.
3. Loop-by-Loop Hand-Linking: We eliminate bulky toe seams by linking every individual knit loop by hand, producing 100% flat, blister-proof closures.
