Privacy Policy & Client Data Discretion

Effective Date: January 1, 2026•KnitFootprint Privacy Standards

1. Commitment to Patron Discretion and Confidentiality

At KnitFootprint, we maintain the highest standards of personal privacy, discretion, and data security for all luxury patrons, bespoke hosiery clients, and textile research partners. We strictly observe data minimization principles compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and global digital governance standards.

2. Scope and Nature of Information Collected

To process bespoke knitwear commissions, coordinate private salon appointments, and distribute textile research monographs, we collect essential professional details (including full name, private email address, phone number, and commission notes). Technical device telemetry, such as pseudonymized IP addresses and browser rendering parameters, is gathered solely to maintain secure SSL/TLS connections and optimize interactive simulation performance.

3. Lawful Purpose of Data Processing

Personal and technical data is utilized exclusively to respond to commission inquiries, coordinate private salon appointments, and deliver bespoke knitwear research monographs. We never deploy client data for automated behavioral profiling, intrusive ad tracking networks, or unsolicited commercial marketing campaigns.

4. Absolute Guarantee Against Commercial Data Sale

KnitFootprint enforces an absolute institutional guarantee: we never sell, lease, monetize, or publicly disclose client records or patron contact lists to third-party marketing syndicates, ad networks, or data brokers. Information is shared solely with essential operational infrastructure partners—such as secure cloud hosting providers and encrypted email delivery systems—under strict non-disclosure obligations.

5. Cryptographic Security Architecture and Data Retention

All digital communications and technical inquiry transmissions are protected using modern TLS 1.3 encryption protocols during transit and AES-256 military-grade encryption at rest. Patron records are maintained securely within isolated databases, accessible solely by authorized atelier directors.

6. Universal User Privacy Rights and Contact

Patrons maintain full legal rights to review, update, export, or request the immediate erasure of their personal records at any time. To exercise your privacy rights or discuss our data stewardship protocols, please contact our Data Governance Officer at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or telephone our concierge desk at +1-888-777-5845.
